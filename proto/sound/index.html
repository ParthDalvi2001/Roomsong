Roomsong - sound prototype

Six plants on a 5-ring x 16-step bed. A ring of light sweeps the bed; each plant sings when it is touched. Press Play, listen for at least 30 seconds, then press "Next room" to hear the same garden re-tuned by a different room: the key, the scale and the echo change. Tempo is yours to set; the room does not change it. Click an empty cell to plant, click a plant to remove it.

Room
  • Bell (FM, decays)
  • Pad (detuned saws, slow)
  • Pluck (plucked string)
  • Tide (current step)
  • Inner ring = low, outer ring = high. Step 1 is at the top, clockwise.
