Roomsong - device capability spike

One tap, hands only. The probe asks the headset what it supports, runs by itself for about 30 seconds, ends the session and shows the results here. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  1. Tap Start and answer what the headset asks.
  2. When the view turns green, pinch once in front of you (thumb and index finger). You hear a click.
  3. Then six steps of sound play, with 8, 16, 24, 32, 48 and 64 voices. Remember the step at which the sound starts to crackle.
  4. The results appear on this page. Answer the three questions above the table.

Run it four times: allowing everything, again after a reload, again after closing and re-opening the browser, and last declining the room permission. The full steps are in the repository, in the file proto/spike/README.md.
