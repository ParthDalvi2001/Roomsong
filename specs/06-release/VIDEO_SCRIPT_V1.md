**Ground rules.** 88.6 s (124 beats at 84 BPM: a beat is 0.71 s, a bar 2.86 s; each cut lands on a beat of its take, so a time may move by up to 0.36 s). Captions only, no voice-over (D-060 (17)); the app's music is the only sound. Burned-in captions bottom centre, at least 48 px at 1080p, at most two lines, at least 1.5 s each: the app's own captions sit just below the frame's centre, so the two never meet. Everything is recorded from the **live site**, with no `?flags`, `?access` or `dev=1`: what is shown is what a judge gets.

## 1. Shot table

Seat for every shot unless the row says otherwise: chair pulled in, back against it, eyes about 45 cm above the table top, the bed where the app puts it (centre 35 to 45 cm ahead), looking down at it about 45° with the whole ring in the middle of the view. The capture is the wearer's view: every head move is a camera move, so turn at most 20° a second and hold still 2 s before and after each take.

## 2. Recording on Quest

- **Dry run, 11 or 12 Nov, each headset (15 min):** record 20 s in the garden and one full V2 rehearsal, copy them to the Mac, and check: the room shows (passthrough is not black), the app's sound is there, no voices, 16:9 at 1080p, smooth. If the room comes out black, stop and tell Parth: then §3 applies to the whole video. Rehearse V2 twice: three plants, a pull, a drag and Listen should fit inside 60 s (the 8 Nov run planted three by 62 s).
- **Capture: the headset's own camera only**, no third-party app, no casting. Quest menu, Camera, its settings (names not yet read off a headset): video landscape 16:9, 1920×1080 if offered, 60 fps if offered, **microphone off**. Start with Record video before pressing Enter; stop it the same way after the take.
- **Headset:** hand tracking on; Do not disturb on (no pop-ups in frame); battery at least 80 %; volume about 70 %; the live GitHub Pages link (not the dev server or `?rec=1`).
- **App:** Access tab: One hand, Look to select, High contrast and Reduce motion all off (the last two hide the bed's glow, BUG-041); Beat ring as shipped (on). Sound tab: Echo on room, Mono off.
- **Both rooms through Space Setup first, on 12 Nov:** walls, floor, ceiling, and the table added as a table (a desk or dining table 50 to 110 cm high, not a coffee table). Room A is the smallest room with a desk, room B the largest with a table (a high ceiling helps). The bigger the size gap, the more the song and the colour differ. In each room read the Sound tab's "Tuned to" line and write it down: those are the V3 labels. If both rooms read the same key, mood and echo, use another room (Parth's).
- **A clean frame:** clear the table; no screens, labels, packaging, book spines; plain sleeves, no logo on a watch or ring; no mirror or glass that shows the wearer; nobody else in the room; a lit room.
- **Order on 13 Nov (Chinmay, Quest 3).** New garden erases the current garden, so first the real-days spare, if any. In room B: V1 (each retake: hold New garden, plant two, record the third), then V2 (each retake: hold New garden, Exit beside Place again, Enter), then V3b, V4a, V4b (Time-lapse off after), the Listen spare. Carry the headset to room A, Enter, V3a. Retakes on 14 Nov: the same order. A seed missed off the bed goes back to the supply: raise the palm again.
- **Parth, Quest 3S, 13 or 14 Nov:** V1, V2, V4 and the Listen spare in his room, as a backup for each; his room is a third room for V3.
- **Files to the Mac.** Parth: USB-C cable, then `adb pull /sdcard/Oculus/VideoShots ~/Movies/SonicSpace-raw` (check the folder name first with `adb shell ls /sdcard/Oculus`). Chinmay: the Meta Horizon phone app's synced captures, or a USB cable to his computer; upload the original files to a shared folder and send Parth the link. Never through a chat app (it recompresses); compare file sizes at both ends. Name files `V2_t3_Q3.mp4`; keep a take log (shot, take, good or not, the second of the first note). Raw video never goes into the repo.

## 3. If a shot fails on the headset

One rule: a shot that fails on both headsets after three takes is replaced by the nearest emulator footage, tagged **"(emulator)"** bottom left for the whole shot, and the end card line and the YouTube description say which shot. V3: the room tour clips of `table` against `large` (ROOM_LOOKS #0; they are silent, so the labels carry the key, mood and echo and the tag reads "(emulator, no sound)"), or gallery image 05. V4b: gallery image 01 (full bloom, made with Time-lapse), tagged "(emulator) time-lapse". V1 and V2 have no stand-in: retake on 14 Nov; the last resort is rough cut v0's first loop, tagged.

## 4. Before upload: tick all 10

1. Length 1:28 to 1:29 (under 1:30); the loop by 0:10; V2 is 60 s with no cut.
2. Every shot is headset footage or tagged "(emulator)"; nothing visible has changed in the build since it was shot (else reshoot it).

6. V3 labels match the "Tuned to" lines written down in each room.
