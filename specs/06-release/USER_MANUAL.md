# Roomsong — Player Guide

**Status:** Living · **Owner:** product-owner · Also published as the in-app Help page (landing → Help).
Written for players. Short, friendly, no jargon.
*For the team: lines marked [if built] describe features that may not ship (wall vines, the daily seed and growth, the browser garden window). At the feature freeze each is unmarked or removed. Nothing here has been checked on a headset yet.*

---

## What is Roomsong?
A tiny glowing garden that lives on your table. Every plant sings. Where you plant decides when it plays and how high it sounds. Your room decides the key. Grow a song with your hands, then lean back and listen.

## Before you start
- Meta Quest 3 or 3S, with **hand tracking on** (Settings → Movement tracking → Hand tracking).
- Put your controllers down. You won't need them.
- You'll see your own real hands. Roomsong doesn't draw hands over them.
- Sit at a table or desk if you can. (No table? It still works: the garden will float in front of you.)

## Your first minute
1. Open the link in the Quest Browser and tap **Enter the garden**.
2. Look at your table. The garden bed appears as a glowing ring.
3. Watch the ripple: it crosses your table, your wall and your ceiling, and each one rings a note, with a short label. That's your room tuning the garden.
4. **Open your hand, palm up.** A seed lands in it.
5. **Pinch the seed and hold it over the ring.** The spot it will land on glows. **Let go**, and it grows into a plant that sings when the light passes.
6. Plant two more. That's a song.

## The gestures
| Do this | What happens |
|---|---|
| ✋ Open palm up | A new seed comes to you (when you have one) |
| 🤏 Pinch a seed, release over the bed | Plant it |
| 🤏 + ↕ Pull a plant up or down | Louder and longer, or quieter and shorter (it changes from where it was) |
| 🤏 + drag | Move it: around the ring changes *when* it plays; in or out changes *how high* |
| 🤏 + drag off the ring (to the side or towards you), let go | Remove it (pinch the floating petal within 5 seconds to undo) |
| 🙌 Both palms out, hold (or pinch the glowing orb in the middle of the ring) | **Listen mode**: lights dim, a glow rises over your garden, and it performs. *[if built]* If a wall is near, vines of light climb it |
| 🤚 Palm toward your face, or any pinch | Wake up from Listen mode |

**The glow shows what you're about to touch.** Before you pinch, the plant or the spot nearest your fingertips lights up. Nothing changes until you let go, so you can always move away first.

**If your hand leaves the headset's view** while you're holding something, the garden waits a moment for you. Bring your hand back quickly and you carry on. If not, the plant slides back to where it was (a seed goes back to wait for you) and a small hint shows until the headset can see your hand again. Nothing is lost.

## Your room tunes the garden
When the garden appears, it reads the shape of your room. Three things in the song come from it:
- **The key** (how low or high the whole song sits) comes from how big the room is. Bigger rooms sing lower.
- **The mood** (which five notes the rings play: bright, soft or open) comes from how high the ceiling is.
- **The echo** comes from how much space the room holds. A small room sounds close; a big room rings longer.

Your table and walls choose which seeds a new garden starts with, and its colour tint. The **speed** is yours: the room never changes it (Settings → Sound). The ripple you see at the start rings one note of your room's chord on each surface. The same room gives the same key and mood every time; now and then the echo can come out a little shorter or longer. Take the garden to another room and the same plants play a new song. (No table or walls found? You still get a song: the garden plays in its "wanderer" key.)

## Come back tomorrow
Your garden is saved on your headset and comes back to the same spot on your table. If the headset can't find that spot (a new room, or the room was set up again), the garden simply finds your table again; your plants are safe. *[if built]* One new seed arrives every day, and plants grow a little as the days pass.

## Settings (pinch the small orb near your wrist)
- **Sound:** speed (yours to set; the room doesn't change it), volume, room echo (auto, small or off), and "Tuned by" (what your room set).
- **Access:** one-hand mode, eyes-only mode (look and hold to select and to listen; pick a short, medium or long hold), high contrast, reduce motion, beat ring, bed height, left/right hand.
- **Garden:** move the bed, re-tune to this room, or start a new garden.

## Try it in a browser (no headset) *[if built]*
Open the same link in a normal browser on a computer and you get the garden window: the same garden on a drawn table. Click an empty spot to plant, drag a plant to move it, drag it off the ring to remove it, click the petal to undo, click the orb in the middle to listen. Two things need a headset: you can't make a plant taller or shorter with a mouse, and there is no settings panel (use the switches on the start page). Your room doesn't tune this garden; it plays in the "wanderer" key.

## Tips
- Everything sounds good together on purpose. You can't play a wrong note.
- Sit so the bed is within easy reach. If it's too far, use **Garden → Re-place bed**.
- Listen mode ends by itself after 10 minutes with a goodnight chord.
- Keep your hands in front of you, above the table, where the headset can see them.
- Nothing in the garden flashes quickly. For even calmer visuals turn on **Access → reduce motion**.

## Privacy
Everything stays on your headset. Roomsong never connects to the internet after it loads.

## Trouble?
- **Nothing happens when I tap Enter:** turn on hand tracking, then reload.
- **No garden appears:** look at your table for a few seconds, or pinch where you want it. If nothing works, it will float in front of you.
- **No sound:** check the headset volume; tap Enter again.
- **A plant jumped back while I was moving it:** your hand left the headset's view. Bring it back in front of you and try again.
- **The song sounds different today:** you are probably in a different room. That's the room re-tuning the garden. In the same room only the echo can shift a little. Use **Garden → Re-tune** to redo it.
