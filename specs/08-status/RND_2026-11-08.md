# R&D pass: voice, special awards, name, hours (8 Nov 2026)
**Owner:** product-owner · desk research only; device checks are for Parth. Sources [n] at the end.

## 1. Voice on Meta Quest Browser
- **Version:** Meta's release notes list 154.1 (30 Oct 2026) as the newest; no v156 found [5] (our headset logs report OculusBrowser/156.0). Meta's specs page warns its example user agent (Chrome/136) is not a feature contract [4].
- **SpeechRecognition / webkitSpeechRecognition:** no Meta page lists it as supported or not [3][4][5][17]. In Chrome the default engine is server-based: "your audio is sent to a web service … it won't work offline" [1][2]. On-device mode (`processLocally = true`) needs a language pack fetched by `install()` [2]. Present on Quest? **Unconfirmed: needs a device check.**
- **getUserMedia({audio:true}):** HTTPS only, with a permission prompt [6]. Works in a Quest page: **unconfirmed: needs a device check.** Keeps working inside an immersive WebXR session: **unconfirmed: needs a device check.**
- **System Voice Commands pressing a page button:** no Meta source found (the help pages tried were 404). **Unconfirmed: needs a device check.**
- **5-minute test for Parth (Quest Browser):**
  1. Open [7]; press Start recognition and say "blue". Background turns blue = recognition exists. Repeat with Wi-Fi off: if it stops, it is server-based.
  2. Open [8]; press Start and allow the microphone. The Instant bar moves when you talk = the microphone works in a page.
  3. Only with the Mac's `chrome://inspect` over USB: on our site, paste into the console
     `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia({audio:true}).then(s=>{const t=s.getAudioTracks()[0];setInterval(()=>console.log('mic',t.readyState,'muted',t.muted),1000)},e=>console.log('mic error',e.name))`
     then Enter the garden. Still `live` and `muted false` inside the session = the track stays open (this does not prove sound gets through).
  4. On our landing page say the system wake phrase, then "Enter the garden" / "click Enter". Note what happens.
- **★ Devpost: say nothing about voice.** Recognition by default is a network service (Golden Rule 9), the build uses none, and the theme is hands-first. No criterion or award gains from it. At most a post-freeze idea.

## 2. Special awards
The Devpost site was unreachable on 8 Nov (HTTP 403 [16]), so this list is from HACKATHON_RULES §7 (Devpost read 9 and 15 Oct; unchanged 27 Oct). $25,000 each, one winner each, open to every track and division: Best Social & Multiplayer · Best Agentic Interaction · Best Reason to Come Back · **Best First Five Minutes** · Best Accessibility Forward · Boldest Original Concept; plus Judge's Choice ($20,000 × 2). One prize per entry. The wording of each award's criteria is not recorded in `specs/`; re-read it at the next weekly rule check (T-090).

**★ Best First Five Minutes.**
- Every judge plays the first five minutes. Reason to Come Back needs a day 2 (only Time-lapse fakes it), and Boldest Original Concept rests on room tuning, which a device has now shown working only after a Space Setup scan (Chinmay's Quest 3, 8 Nov).
- The app is built for it: first sound ≤ 20 s, a three-plant loop ≤ 90 s, the guided first minute and the tuning ripple. On the 7 Nov headset run, three seeds were planted in 38 s once the palm pick was on (D-062); on Chinmay's 8 Nov run, all 3 by 62 s.
- Weak spot: the 7 Nov run showed the guide skipping ahead (BUG-022, D-061). A stranger's run on a device before the freeze is the evidence that counts.

**Wording:** put "Your first minute" second, with times: "Press Enter: in about N s your room plays its first note; three pinches make a song within 90 s. No account, no menus, no controllers." Device numbers only if measured, else mark them "(emulator)". In the video, show the first 60 s without cuts.

## 3. Name check: SonicSpace
No exact "SonicSpace" / "Sonic Space" on the Meta Horizon Store [9], Steam [10], Google Play [11] or itch.io [12]. Apple has "Emphasis: Sonic Spaces", a productivity app [13]. Trademarkia shows 0 marks for SONICSPACE [14]. For "sonic space", the first page shows only live SONIC marks in other classes (food, tools, toothbrushes) [15].
In stores, "Sonic" is SEGA's Sonic the Hedgehog games (five of the first 18 Play results), and a music app is called "SONIC" [11].
Not checked: EUIPO, WIPO, the Indian register, a full USPTO search, web search (blocked).
**Risk: medium** (low on registered marks, medium on how judges and Meta read it: "Sonic" in a game context). The name also drops "room", the core idea. **★ Keep Roomsong;** if renamed, rename everywhere (in-app title, landing page, video, gallery, and the `/Roomsong/` URL) so judges see one name.
