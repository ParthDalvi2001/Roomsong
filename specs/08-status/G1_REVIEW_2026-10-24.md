## 3. Suggested verdict: conditional pass

The emulator two-thirds of the gate is met; the headset third cannot be judged. The plan foresaw this (Emergency mode, D-013), so the honest reading is a pass on what can be measured, conditional on:

1. **One clean full browser run at the G1 commit** (the driver; the last was 20 Oct, 107/107; the heap soak apart). A fail here reopens the gate.
2. **The headset (T-000):** a date from Parth, or Emergency mode declared on 27 Oct as D-013 says; the three device criteria move to G2 unchanged and the Devpost "Known limitations" keeps the emulator-only alternative until a sitting happens.
3. **Go live (T-108):** Parth's six steps of item 3 this week; before 27 or 29 Oct if item 42 (Playtest & React) is a yes, since that is the only outside Quest play before the freeze.
4. **The sound style (item 35):** picked by ear; the G0 sound criterion stays failed until a rating ≥ 4/5 from Parth and ≥ 3/5 from Chinmay and two outsiders exists.
5. **The draws criterion** restated with its view count ("≤ 30 for both views"), or the measured 6–12 per view accepted as meeting "≤ 15".

Alternatives: **pass** (reading the headset lines as void without a headset) understates what is unknown; **fail** (reading them as misses) would stop nothing, since every open item is a human one and the code work continues either way. Conditional pass is what G0's suggested verdict was too; both are recorded, not held, until Parth answers.

## 4. Room-signature go/no-go draft

**What is being decided.** S1 (PRD §S1, narrowed by D-021): room features → signature v1 (key root, mode, reverb; the starter voices; a palette seed) → the tuning ripple → re-tune. The Wanderer tuning (C major pentatonic, 84 BPM, 0.9 s reverb, Bell / Pad / Pluck) whenever the bed is at level 3 or 4 or a feature is unknown (TC-SIG-04). Built and merged: T-140, T-141, T-142 / 142b, T-143; unit TC-SIG-01..04 green (determinism, ±5 cm / ±0.3 m² stability, three fixture rooms → three signatures by hand, the fallback); `17-ripple`; `29-room-matrix` (six rooms place and play, which proves the chain's robustness, not the signature's truth on a device). It is the differentiator (D-010) and a Should only because no headset can verify it.

**The default, and this draft's call: GO.** The T-130 row, PRD Q7 and the G2 criteria all set the default with no T-129 evidence: *build with the Wanderer fallback, planes optional.* Read as: the signature stays in the build; the app never requires planes (every level of the fallback chain plays the full game); the room is a bonus layer that is silent when scene data is missing; the Devpost sentences marked `[not yet: T-155]` (six places) stay marked until G2. Item 41 (keep the walls' signature when the bed floats over a low table) is the same reading of "planes optional" and is recommended yes.

**Evidence that confirms GO (and lets T-155 remove the markers), from DEVICE_SCRIPT_S1:**
- Block 1 (TC-DEV-0A, four runs): planes **and semantic labels** report *working* in Quest Browser on a Quest 3 (3S if available): table, wall and ceiling labels seen, plane counts early and late, +Y per surface, and the permission-decline run returning empty sets without errors (P2).
- Block 10: the bed at level 1 or 2 in a Space Setup room; block 7: a room without Space Setup goes to Wanderer with the first sound ≤ 20 s (item 23 / 39 (a)).
- Block 6 (same room, second session) and block 16: the same key and mode on a second visit; the drift of a room near a bucket edge is "about one room in five" by calculation (item 21) and unmeasured.
- Block 15 (a key change listened to) and block 17 (rooms against the tape): a listener hears the room's difference (Parth ≥ 3/5) and the measured lengths sit inside TC-SIG-02's tolerance.

**Evidence that flips it to NO-GO (out, Wanderer in every room):** planes or labels not supported, not granted, or not working in Quest Browser (a table that cannot be told from a counter or a floor); the signature changing between two sessions in the demo room, or in more than one room in five; the room's difference inaudible to Parth and the outsiders (≤ 2/5); the plane wait keeping the first sound past 20 s with no fix accepted; or an S1 bug in the chain inside the last week before the freeze. "Out" costs about a day: force `WANDERER` in `room/` (one config switch), cut the ripple captions that promise a tint (item 39 (b)), remove the six marked Devpost sentences and move the pitch to the garden that grows, Listen and the table as the stage (DEVPOST_DRAFT §5, "a pitch with no room signature").

**With no headset by G2 (Emergency mode from 27 Oct):** the default holds, GO, and the Devpost says exactly what was device-tested and what was not (Golden Rule 11): the signature unverified on a headset; the emulator's six rooms give six placements and the three fixtures three signatures by hand. Playtest & React (item 42) is the one chance of outside Quest evidence before the freeze: ask the players for the ripple's captions and the panel's "Tuned by" line (a screenshot each): if a stranger's Quest reads a table and walls, the labels work.

**Decided by:** G2, Monday 3 Nov (T-155; PRD Q7, "scrum-master + Parth", with Chinmay's outsider scoring). 3 Nov is also the last day the cut fits comfortably before the feature freeze of 12 Nov. Until then nothing in the chain is cut and nothing new is added to it.
