## Key question 1b — Plan for using a friend's headset for one hour (what to prepare, how to launch the link, how to record and get the recording off)

### Takeaway
A public HTTPS link opened in Meta Quest Browser needs no Developer Mode (Developer Mode is only referenced for ADB port-forwarding during local development); the headset's built-in recorder captures what the wearer sees and the recording can be synced to the Meta Horizon phone app or meta.com/gallery, or copied over USB (Windows natively; Mac needs an MTP tool).

### Cited Findings
- WebXR requires a secure origin: "This is required for WebXR to work - browsers only allow WebXR on secure origins." Testing on a physical Quest over LAN uses the dev server's IP URL; the ADB port-forwarding alternative is the only step that says to "enable developer mode on the headset". Entering the experience is "simply click 'Enter XR'". — [Meta IWSDK: Testing your experience](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/iwsdk/guides/02-testing-experience/)
- Web Launch lets a site send a URL to the headset: format `https://www.oculus.com/open_url/?url=<encoded-https-url>`; "Web Launch targets must use HTTPS." — [Meta: Web Launch](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/web/web-launch)
- Link Sharing: open the page in the phone browser, use Share → Meta (Oculus) app → "open immediately" (opens in the headset browser) or "save" (appears under Discover → Recently Saved). — [MIXED: Link sharing on Meta Quest](https://mixed-news.com/en/link-sharing-on-meta-quest-2-what-is-it-and-how-does-it-work/)
- Hand tracking is enabled under Navigator → Quick controls → Settings → Tracking → "Hand and Body Tracking". Palm pinch to open the menu with hands: "Look at your palm at eye level, then hold your thumb and index finger together until Oculus button fills, then release." Point and pinch: "point your hand at what you want to select. Then, pinch your thumb and finger together to select." — [Meta Help: Hand and Body Tracking](https://www.meta.com/help/quest/290147772643252/)
- Recording: "Press Meta button or Oculus button on your right controller to open the Navigator. Select Camera, then select Record video." Shortcut: hold the Meta button and "press and hold the right trigger to start recording". "A red dot will appear in your headset to indicate that recording has started." Videos are "in Files (under the Media or Recents folder), or in the Gallery app." — [Meta Help: How to record video](https://www.meta.com/help/quest/516228089650875/)
- Sync to phone/web: "Select Settings. Select Camera. Next to Sync photos and videos, turn the toggle to the on position." Conditions: "your headset needs to be on and idle. The sync will fail if you open another app, if your headset disconnects from Wi-Fi or if your headset turns off." "Videos taken from your headset that are over a minute long need to be individually synced." Download from the Meta Horizon app Gallery ("Tap Download") or from meta.com/gallery ("Click the menu button, then click Download"). — [Meta Help: Sync media](https://www.meta.com/help/quest/317431133450056/)
- USB (Windows only in Meta's article): connect with USB-C, "select Allow", then copy from "This PC\[Your Quest Device]\Internal shared storage\Oculus\VideoShots". The article states the requirement "A computer running Windows." — [Meta Help: Transfer media to computer](https://www.meta.com/help/quest/308605304163937/)
- On macOS the headset is not mounted natively (MTP); users rely on Android File Transfer or third-party tools (MacDroid etc.), with mixed reports. — [MacDroid guide](https://www.macdroid.app/connect-oculus-to-mac/); [Meta forum thread on Android File Transfer](https://communityforums.atmeta.com/discussions/dev-unity/cant-not-copy-file-to-mac-via-android-file-transfer/723246)
- OVR Metrics Tool "CSV recording is currently not supported for Browser (including webXR)" per a Meta forum reply; fps evidence for WebXR must come from the on-screen overlay or in-app counters. — [Meta forum: OVR Metrics Tool CSV for WebXR](https://communityforums.atmeta.com/discussions/WebXRDevelopment/ovr-metrics-tool-no-csv-recordings-for-webxr-application/1271550)
- A Quest app vendor's help page confirms the system recorder captures "video of what you see in VR" and saves it to the Files app. — [SketchUp for Meta Quest: Recording your session](https://help.sketchup.com/en/meta-quest/recording-your-session)

### Inferences
- One-hour checklist derived from the above: (1) deploy a frozen build to a public HTTPS URL; (2) print a QR code that encodes the Web Launch URL `https://www.oculus.com/open_url/?url=<encoded build URL>` so the friend scans it on their phone (logged into the Meta Horizon app) and the page opens in the headset, with a short typed URL as fallback; (3) before putting the headset on, confirm hand tracking is on, "Sync photos and videos" is on, and the friend's Horizon app is logged in; (4) start recording from the Navigator before "Enter XR", keep each clip under ~1 minute if relying on auto-sync, otherwise sync clips individually; (5) also capture an fps overlay in-app because OVR Metrics CSV export is unavailable for browser content; (6) leave the headset on and idle on Wi-Fi for sync, or copy VideoShots over USB (Windows laptop is simpler than macOS).
- Because judges must evaluate "the state of the Entry at the time of submission deadline" (see Key question 4), the friend-test URL should be a staging URL distinct from the final submission URL.
- Passthrough recordings will show the friend's room; any posters, product logos or family photos in frame are a content-rule risk in the video (see Key question 4), so clear the table and walls before recording.

### Gaps
- I found no official Meta page confirming in-headset QR-code scanning from Meta Quest Browser; the QR plan therefore routes through the phone (Web Launch or Link Sharing).
- No official Meta page states a maximum recording length or resolution for the built-in recorder.
- Whether Meta's "Developer Access"/Developer Mode is needed for anything in a pure browser flow is inferred from the IWSDK guide's omission, not stated explicitly.

## Key question 2 — Does the 2026 rules text explicitly allow emulator/XR-Simulator footage for the video? Do judges test on device?

### Takeaway
Yes: the Official Rules require footage "as viewed on a Meta Quest device or via XR Simulator or another equivalent emulator", although the Overview page shortens this to "as viewed on a Meta VR device". The rules never state that judges will test on a headset, but they require a link "for judges to access and test your application", require access "to enable viewing, evaluation, use, and testing by Sponsor and Judges", and score Technical Implementation on "min 60 fps" on Meta Quest hardware.

### Cited Findings
- Official Rules, Entry Requirements (verbatim): "Demonstration video of your Project that should be less than three (3) minutes and showcase footage of your Project as viewed on a Meta Quest device or via XR Simulator or another equivalent emulator. The video must be uploaded to and made publicly visible on YouTube or Vimeo and a link to the video must be provided on the submission form on the Competition website. Judges are not required to watch beyond three minutes." — [Official Rules](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/rules)
- Overview page (verbatim, narrower): "A demonstration video—under three (3) minutes, showcasing footage of your Project as viewed on a Meta VR device". — [Competition overview](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/)
- Official Rules on WebXR access (verbatim): "For apps built with IWSDK (WebXR): A link will be required for judges to access and test your application. See 'Deploying to GitHub Pages' for additional details." — [Official Rules](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/rules)
- Official Rules on access (verbatim): "Projects must be made available free of charge and with sufficient access until the Winner Announcement to enable viewing, evaluation, use, and testing by Sponsor and Judges (as defined below)." — [Official Rules](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/rules)
- Official Rules, Technical Implementation (verbatim fragment): "High-scoring entries must be performant on Meta Quest hardware (min 60 fps), solid and bug-free". — [Official Rules](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/rules)
- Official Rules on judging (verbatim): "Entries will be evaluated based on the Judging criteria listed below by a panel of experts designated by Sponsor"; "AI tools may also be used to assist in the judging, but final decisions will be made by human judges."; "In the event of a tie, it will be resolved by Judges' vote." Two stages: Stage One is a pass/fail viability check that the project fits the theme; Stage Two scores the four equally weighted criteria. — [Official Rules](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/rules); [Judging page](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/details/judging)
- FAQ on third-party devices (verbatim): "Can I use third-party non-Meta devices e.g. bluetooth accessories?" — "No. Testing should work only on Meta VR devices that judges have equal access to." — [FAQ](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/details/faqs)
- Video honesty guidance (verbatim): "On your video/trailer: show real gameplay that honestly represents the experience. Lead with the story and what makes your project distinct. Don't lean on AI-generated video to carry the pitch." — [Official Rules](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/rules)
- Meta's announcement names "Immersive Web Emulator" and "Meta XR Simulator" as supported tools for the competition. — [Meta blog: competition announcement](https://developers.meta.com/blog/meta-connect-2026-vr-start-developer-competition/)

### Inferences
- The rules text governs over the Overview summary, so emulator (IWER) footage is permitted; but the "honestly represents the experience" guidance and the device-bound 60 fps criterion mean an emulator-only video and an untested build will likely score lower on Technical Implementation and Polish & Presentation than a device-recorded one.
- "A link will be required for judges to access and test your application" plus the FAQ's "devices that judges have equal access to" strongly implies judges open the WebXR link on a Quest; the team should assume on-device testing in rooms it never saw (the rules explicitly reward "holding up in rooms the developer never tested", per the project's rules digest).

### Gaps
- The rules do not say how many judges test each entry, how long they spend, on which Quest model, or whether they test hands-first flows end-to-end; no organiser clarification about WebXR testing was found (the Discussions page rendered without threads through the proxy).

## Key question 3 — What exactly must the Devpost submission contain for a WebXR entry, and is there a "testing instructions" field?

### Takeaway
The rules list five required submission elements (build link, under-3-minute public video, text description covering inspiration/how built/future plans/target launch date, Track, Division); there is no dedicated "testing instructions" field in the rules, so any no-table-room or permissions guidance must go into the text description and the video.

### Cited Findings
- Official Rules (verbatim list): "All required items must be added to the Submission of the Project on Devpost which includes, but is not limited to: Link to your Project in the Competition release channel (from the Meta VR Developer Dashboard) or to your GitHub page (if built with IWSDK / WebXR). Demonstration video of your Project that should be less than three (3) minutes and showcase footage of your Project as viewed on a Meta Quest device or via XR Simulator or another equivalent emulator... A text description that explains your inspiration, how you built it, future plans for improvement and a target launch date, if not already published to the Meta VR Store. If your Project is an Adapted / Significantly Updated Experience (as described above), Entry should include a summary description of the new features/capabilities added to the application. Screenshots, changelogs and/or demo videos are highly recommended. Select the Track you are submitting your Project into: Entertainment, Gaming, or Productivity. Select the Division you are submitting your Project into: New Experience or Adapted / Significantly Updated Experience." — [Official Rules](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/rules)
- Official Rules: "Complete and enter all of the required fields on the 'Enter a Submission' page of the Contest Website during the Entry Period and follow the requirements below." and "Entries must be submitted in full (all steps above must be completed) by Entrant during the Entry Period in strict accordance with the instructions and restrictions in these Official Rules and any other instructions that Sponsor may provide through its authorized representatives or otherwise." — [Official Rules](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/rules)
- Overview page lists the form as including "Submission name, Submission tagline (140 characters), Track: Entertainment, Gaming, or Productivity" and describes the build as "APK via Meta VR Developer Dashboard with Invite URL, or GitHub Pages link for WebXR projects". — [Competition overview](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/)
- FAQ: "What submission information do I need to select when submitting my project?" — projects require an APK upload (or GitHub link for WebXR), demonstration video, description, and division/track selection; "Is it mandatory to include a video of our project in the submission?" — "Yes, a video is required as part of submission." — [FAQ](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/details/faqs)
- FAQ on tools (verbatim): "All development tools, SDKs, and features used must be available to the general public and/or Meta VR Start Program members." and "You may use Unity, Unreal, Godot, Native/Android, Immersive Web SDK or another engine." — [FAQ](https://start-developer-competition-26.devpost.com/details/faqs)
- Devpost's own help centre notes that after the deadline "you can't change a submission's video, but you can still change the one on your project page", and that a "Submission history" section records which video was submitted. — [Devpost Help: How to edit a submission](https://help.devpost.com/article/123-how-to-edit-a-submission)

### Inferences
- The rules say "GitHub page" / "GitHub Pages link" for WebXR; hosting on GitHub Pages matches the rule text literally, whereas another static host (e.g., Vercel) is not prohibited but departs from the wording. Prefer GitHub Pages as the submitted link.
- Since no testing-instructions field exists, put a short "How to test" block at the top of the description: required permissions, "works without a table — falls back to floor/wall anchors", hand-tracking must be on, seated use, expected first-minute flow, and the fps overlay toggle. The project's existing SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST should carry this.
- The description is read by both AI tools and human judges ("AI tools may also be used to assist in the judging"), so a structured, keyword-dense first paragraph is useful.

### Gaps
- The exact live Devpost form fields (e.g., whether a "Try it out" link list, "Built with" tags, or image gallery exist for this contest) could not be seen without logging in; the rules' "includes, but is not limited to" wording means extra fields may exist.

## Key question 4 — India eligibility, Start timing, team/Representative rules, New Experience proof, content-policy traps, no-changes enforcement, prize/tax logistics

### Takeaway
India is not on the exclusion list (Brazil, Quebec, sanctioned areas), the Representative must be a Start member "by the time of submission", and the New Experience division requires the project to be "conceived of and built within the competition window (starting September 24, 2026)" with no stated evidence format; the biggest practical traps are post-deadline changes to a live WebXR URL, branded objects or other people visible in passthrough footage, and a 30% US withholding on prizes that tax treaties generally do not exempt.

- IRS: Form 1042-S "Foreign Person's U.S. Source Income Subject to Withholding" reports "Income and amounts withheld as described in the Instructions for Form 1042-S", with Publication 515 governing withholding on nonresident aliens. — [IRS: About Form 1042-S](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1042-s)

- A developer-forum post about a different Devpost hackathon reports that Devpost handles payouts and "can take up to 60 days (and sometimes more)"; not specific to this contest. — [monday.com community: hackathon prizes](https://developer-community.monday.com/api-apps-framework-4/hackathon-prizes-838)

### Inferences
- India-specific prize constraints: expect ~30% US withholding on a cash prize paid to an Indian individual, a W-8BEN and government ID in the Winner Documents, a bank account able to receive USD (and the resulting Indian income-tax treatment of foreign prize income, which is outside this research), and payment only after documents clear. The Representative receives the whole prize and must split it; Meta is not involved in the split.
- New Experience proof: with no prescribed evidence, a git history whose first commit is on/after 2026-09-24, dated design notes and a Devpost registration date are the practical defence; any earlier "Bloomtone" artefacts should be documented as concept-only or kept clearly separate.
- Post-deadline risk is sharper for WebXR than for APKs: an APK in a "Competition" release channel is frozen by the dashboard, whereas a GitHub Pages URL can silently change. Freeze the deployment branch at submission, tag it, record the build hash in the description, and do not deploy again until Dec 11.
- Content traps specific to a passthrough MR app: real-room footage in the video can capture "recognizable branded products" (laptops, phones, posters), and any housemate or friend in frame or audible is an "identifiable person other than Entrants". Clear the scene and mute ambient audio before recording.

- Whether Meta's 2026 rules include Devpost's standard "permitted post-deadline fix for infringing/PII material" exception could not be confirmed.
